How SkillHire works

A campus-first marketplace designed to help students learn, earn and collaborate.

👤

1. Build a profile

Students list their skills, showcase experience or sample work, and set affordable starting prices.

⌕

2. Find a match

Buyers search by service or category and compare skills, profiles and prices.

🤝

3. Collaborate

The buyer and student seller agree on deliverables, deadline and project price.

📈

4. Grow together

Students gain practice, portfolio projects, client feedback and confidence for future jobs.
